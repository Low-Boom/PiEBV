## Case 1: CH1 versus CH2 skew (R19 and J10)

U7.2 / U8.2: crossing of the AHCT threshold (1.4 V est; in brackets the skew at 0.8 V and at 2.0 V, the guaranteed band). J7 / J8: 50 % into 50 ohm through 1 m RG-58, all devices typical and identical on both channels, so the J7-J8 skew is from routing and loading only (U13 channel matching, SLUSFA9 tM 1 / 2 ns, comes on top). J10 'open' and '2-3' are the same circuit for CH1 (J10.1 open in both). The GPIO setting applies to GP11, GP12 and GP13. ## Case 5: delay-reduction options

### GP11 drive strength and slew rate: ringing on PWM_Trig / PWM_TRIG_TAP

J10 on 2-3. Pin = J14.15 pad (absolute maximum 3.8 V, RP2350 Table 1680). Ring-back = the lowest value after the first peak of a rising edge (highest after the first dip of a falling edge), taken after the edge has left the receiver's threshold band (U5: above 1.17 V / below 0.63 V; U19: above 2.3 V / below 0.8 V); 'monotonic' = no peak. The value includes a dip inside a stepped edge, not only the ring after the final level (a value well below the final level, above the band, is such a dip). U5 has no hysteresis: 'risk' = the ring-back re-enters its 0.63-1.17 V band, so a camera trigger can fire twice. U19 'risk' = the ring-back passes VIN_L max 1.2 V (rise) or VIN_H min 1.8 V (fall). The U5 input clamp to GND is modelled (est). For the effect of the pad setting on the CH1/CH2 skew, see the 12 mA fast rows of case 1.
